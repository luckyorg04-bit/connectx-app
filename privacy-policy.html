ConnectX Privacy Policy

Effective date: 25 November 2026
App: ConnectX – Chat

This Privacy Policy explains how ConnectX ("ConnectX", "we", "us" or "our"), operated by Ghost Group of Industries, handles information when you use the ConnectX mobile application and related services.

1. Information we may collect

  • Account and profile information: email address, name, username, profile photo and bio that you provide when registering or editing your profile. Authentication may be handled through Firebase Authentication and Google Sign-In.
  • Messages and content: text messages, group messages, polls, images, videos, voice notes, files and other content you choose to send or upload. This content is processed to deliver the communication features and may be stored using our service providers.
  • Calls and permissions: when you use voice or video calling, the app requests microphone and, for video calls, camera access. Audio/video streams are used to connect the call. The app does not need these permissions for ordinary text messaging.
  • Location: if you choose to use a location-sharing feature and grant location permission, the app may access your device location and share the location information with the recipient(s) you select. You can decline permission and avoid using location sharing.
  • Device and notification information: a device or messaging token and technical information needed to deliver push notifications and maintain app functionality.
  • Service and diagnostic information: limited technical records may be processed by the app infrastructure and service providers for authentication, delivery, security, troubleshooting and reliability.

2. How we use information

We use information to create and manage accounts, display profiles, deliver messages and shared files, enable group conversations and calls, send notifications, provide optional location sharing, protect the service, troubleshoot failures and respond to support or deletion requests.

3. How information is shared

  • With other users: information and content you choose to share in a one-to-one chat, group, profile or location-sharing action is visible to the relevant recipients. Think carefully before sharing sensitive information.
  • Service providers: we use Google Firebase services, which may include Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Realtime Database and Firebase Cloud Messaging, to support app functionality. Google Sign-In may be used if you choose that sign-in method.
  • Our infrastructure: content or files you upload may be transmitted to our application upload infrastructure at connecttx.duckdns.org to provide messaging and profile-photo functionality.
  • Legal and safety reasons: we may disclose information where required by applicable law or where reasonably necessary to protect users, the service or legal rights.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use the app to serve third-party advertising.

4. Data storage and security

Information is processed and stored by the app and its service providers as needed to operate the service. We use reasonable technical and organisational safeguards, but no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

5. Data retention

We retain account information and user content for as long as needed to provide the service, meet legitimate operational needs and comply with applicable legal obligations. Some copies may remain temporarily in backups or service-provider systems after deletion while those systems complete their normal deletion cycles.

6. Account and data deletion

You may request deletion of your ConnectX account and associated personal data by emailing contact@luckydubey.in from your registered email address. Include your ConnectX username and write “Account and Data Deletion Request” in the subject. We may ask for reasonable information to verify that you control the account. We will process verified requests in accordance with applicable law and explain if any information must be retained for legal or security reasons.

Important: Sending a deletion request by email is the currently documented request route. Do not assume that uninstalling the app deletes your account or data.

7. Children’s privacy

ConnectX is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can review the request.

8. Your choices

You can manage app permissions through your device settings, choose what content you share, and stop using optional features such as camera, microphone or location sharing. You may contact us to ask about, correct or request deletion of your information, subject to applicable law.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app or our practices change. The latest version will be published at this URL, with the effective date shown at the top.
