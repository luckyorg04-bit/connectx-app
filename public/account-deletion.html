ConnectX Account and Data Deletion

App: ConnectX – Chat
Developer: Ghost Group of Industries

This page explains how to request deletion of your ConnectX account and associated personal data. You do not need to reinstall or sign in to the app to submit a request.

How to submit a request

  1. Email us from the email address registered to your ConnectX account.
  2. Use the subject ConnectX Account and Data Deletion Request.
  3. Include your ConnectX username and any information needed for us to identify the account.
  4. We may request reasonable verification to protect your account from unauthorized deletion.

Email account deletion request

What happens after you submit

ConnectX support will review the request, verify account ownership and process deletion of the account and associated personal data. We will communicate if additional verification is needed or if particular records must be retained for legal, security or fraud-prevention reasons. We aim to process verified requests as quickly as reasonably possible.

Data covered by the request

The request covers the ConnectX account and associated profile and contact information, private phone number, notification/device tokens, uploaded profile and message media, and user-associated content stored by ConnectX, subject to lawful retention exceptions. Content already delivered to other participants may require separate handling and may not be removable from recipients' devices or copies.
